# Notes & To-Dos

<FeatureIntro image="/screenshots/notes.webp" alt="Launch0 Notes: a private chat with yourself, with a to-do, a voice memo and an image">

Launch0 Notes is a private notepad built into your home screen, laid out like a chat with yourself. Swipe left from home, type, and send. Thoughts, to-dos, voice memos and images all land in one running thread, grouped by day.

Nothing to sign in to, nothing to sync: every note stays in Launch0's private storage on your phone.

</FeatureIntro>

## What you can capture

- **Text** — a thought, a link or a reminder, sent like a message. Links and email addresses become tappable.
- **To-dos** — mark any note **done** with its checkbox, or flag it **urgent** so it stands out.
- **Voice memos** — tap the mic to record, then play, pause and resume inline. Each shows its length.
- **Images** — attach one from the picker, or share an image into Notes from any app through Android's share sheet.

Long-press any note to **copy**, **share**, **edit** or **delete** it. Deleting a note also deletes its image or audio file.

## Find anything

Swipe left again inside Notes to open search. Results filter as you type and sit right above the keyboard, so the note you want is within thumb reach.

## Why it lives in the launcher

A notes app is one more icon to find and one more feed to get lost in. In Launch0, Notes is a gesture away from the place you already are every time you unlock your phone. Capture the thought, swipe back, and carry on.

## FAQ

### Where are my notes stored?

Only on your device, in Launch0's private storage. Text and to-dos are saved as app data, and images and voice memos as private files. Launch0 declares no internet permission, so it can't upload them. See the [privacy policy](/privacy#notes).

### Will I lose my notes if I switch between the Play Store and the APK?

Yes, unless you back them up first. The Google Play and GitHub builds are signed with different keys, so switching means uninstalling, and uninstalling deletes Launch0's data. Pick one source and stick with it. See [Download](/download).

### Can I open Notes with a different gesture?

Notes is the default swipe-left action. You can change swipe left to open an app instead in **Settings → Gestures**.
