# Calendar Widget

<FeatureIntro>

Launch0's calendar widget puts the rest of today on your home screen: the events you still have, each with a countdown to when it starts. You see what's next every time you unlock your phone, without opening the calendar app and getting pulled into the week ahead.

It reads events from the calendar already on your phone, on your device. Launch0 declares no internet permission, so your schedule can't go anywhere.

</FeatureIntro>

## Turn it on

1. Long-press the home screen to open **Settings**.
2. Turn on **Calendar widget**.
3. On the home screen, tap the widget and allow calendar access.

## What it shows

- **Today's remaining events**, in order. About two rows show at a time; scroll the card for the rest.
- **A countdown for each event** — "in 15m", "in 1h 5m" — or **now** while it's happening.
- **The event's location**, when it has one.
- **How many events are left today**, in the card's header.

Tap an event to open it in your calendar app, or tap the header to open the calendar.

## What it leaves out

The widget is a "what's next" list, so it skips anything that isn't a timed event still ahead of you:

- Events that have already ended drop off within a minute.
- Cancelled events, rescheduled "ghosts" and events you declined never show.
- All-day entries such as holidays, birthdays and working locations are left out.

If your work profile shares its calendar with your personal profile, work events appear too, and an event that's on two calendars shows once.

## FAQ

### Why is an event missing?

The widget shows only calendars that sync to your phone. If a calendar appears in your calendar app's web view but isn't set to sync on the device, Launch0 can't see it. All-day events are left out by design.

### Can I show it with the days-left widget?

Yes. When more than one widget is on, they share one card under the clock and you swipe between them.

### What does Launch0 do with my calendar?

It reads today's events to draw the widget and keeps them only in memory while they're on screen. It doesn't save them, and with no internet permission it can't send them anywhere. See the [privacy policy](/privacy#calendar-calendar-widget).
